A menu bar snippet board and clipboard history for your Mac. No account, no sync, no analytics — nothing you copy ever leaves your device.

What it does

  • Snippet board — save the text you paste all the time, organize it into groups, pin your favourites.
  • Clipboard history — your recent copies, searchable and ready to paste again.
  • Keyboard-first — ⌥⇧V opens the panel with search focused; ↑/↓ to move, Return to copy, ⌘1–⌘9 for the first nine items.
  • Encrypted local storage — one AES-GCM encrypted file in the app sandbox, key held in your macOS Keychain.
  • Password-safe — copies marked concealed by password managers are never recorded.

Common questions

I launched CopyCat and nothing happened.

CopyCat has no Dock icon or window — it lives in the menu bar. Look for the 🐾 paw icon at the top right of your screen and click it, or press ⌥⇧V from any app.

macOS is asking whether CopyCat can access text from other apps.

macOS 15.4 and later show this prompt once for any app that reads the clipboard. Choose Allow Always — clipboard history can't work otherwise. You can change it later in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Does my clipboard data get uploaded anywhere?

No. There's no account, no sync, no analytics and no server of ours to send it to. CopyCat's only network connection is to Apple's App Store, used solely to show prices for the optional icon unlocks and to complete a purchase you start — nothing from your clipboard is part of that.

Are passwords saved in the history?

No. Password managers mark their copies with the standard concealed / transient / auto-generated clipboard types, and CopyCat skips anything carrying those markers.

How do I change the shortcut, or stop recording history?

Open the panel, click the gear icon. There you can record a new shortcut, pause monitoring, set the history limit (25–200), turn on auto-delete after an hour or a day, clear history, or export your snippets as JSON.

Where is my data stored, and how do I remove it?

In the app's sandbox container at ~/Library/Containers/wutheringstudios.CopyCat/, encrypted. Deleting CopyCat removes it. The encryption key is a Keychain item named wutheringstudios.CopyCat, which you can delete in Keychain Access.
