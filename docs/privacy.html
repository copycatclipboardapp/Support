Privacy Policy

Effective 23 July 2026

CopyCat ("the app") is a macOS menu bar app developed by Wuthering Studios ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your data. The short version: nothing you copy ever leaves your Mac, and we never receive any of it.

Data we collect

None. CopyCat has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no third-party trackers. We do not receive, see or store any information about you or your use of the app.

Why that is verifiable, not just a promise

CopyCat runs in the macOS App Sandbox and is shipped without the network entitlement. macOS itself prevents the app from opening any network connection — the capability simply isn't granted to it. There is no server component to send data to.

Data the app stores on your Mac

  • Snippets and groups you create.
  • Clipboard history — text you copy while monitoring is on, capped at your chosen limit (25–200 items, default 100).
  • Settings — your shortcut, history limit, auto-delete choice and optional background image.

Snippets, groups and history are written to a single file inside the app's sandbox container, encrypted with AES-GCM. The encryption key is generated on your Mac and stored only in your macOS Keychain; the file cannot be read without it. Settings are stored in the app's own preferences within the same container.

What the app never records

  • Clipboard items marked org.nspasteboard.ConcealedType, TransientType or AutoGeneratedType — the markers password managers such as 1Password, Bitwarden and Apple Passwords apply to copied credentials.
  • Non-text clipboard content (images, files).
  • Anything at all while "Pause clipboard monitoring" is switched on.

Your control over the data

  • Clear the entire history at any time from Settings.
  • Turn on auto-delete so history is discarded after an hour or a day.
  • Delete individual snippets or history items from the panel.
  • Export snippets to a JSON file you choose the location of — that file is written unencrypted, so store it somewhere you trust.
  • Deleting the app removes its container and everything in it. The Keychain item named wutheringstudios.CopyCat can be removed separately using Keychain Access.

Children

CopyCat is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever gains a feature that changes any of the above, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date at the top will change.
